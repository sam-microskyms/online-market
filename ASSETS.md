# Sample media

Nothing here is stock this shop holds. The reel plays three filmed clips. Other listings are photographs. Older mp4 files that only move across a still are not played in the reel.

## Filmed reel clips

Each file was transcoded to H.264 with AAC audio, scaled down, and given a JPEG poster from one frame. The kettle is the whole source clip (about 5.3 seconds; the source is shorter than 15). The two mug files are 15-second trims. They are not looped copies and not pans of a still.
